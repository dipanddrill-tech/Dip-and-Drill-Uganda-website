Our Identity

Complete Water Solutions, Not Just Drilling

Dip and Drill Uganda Limited is a Ugandan water solutions company providing groundwater surveys, borehole drilling, pumping systems, storage, irrigation, piping, water quality support, and maintenance.

Our approach is practical and complete. We assess the water source, build the borehole, install pumping systems, plan storage, support irrigation, handle piping, test water quality, and continue supporting the client after completion.

This makes us a dependable partner for household water supply, institutional supply, community water access, and farm productivity.
